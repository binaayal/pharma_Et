| Price | Free (the subscription is billed outside the stores) | Free |

**Why the subscription is not an in-app purchase.** PharmaEt is software that a business
buys for its own operations, billed by bank transfer and verified by a person. Apple's
guideline 3.1.3(f) ("Free Stand-alone Apps"), 3.1.3(c) (Enterprise Services) and Google
Play's payments policy exempt payment for business services consumed outside the app. The
app never shows a price with a "buy" button. It shows where to transfer and lets the
owner upload the receipt. Keep it that way: adding an in-app "Pay now" card flow would bring
the subscription under store billing.

**Full description, English**

> PharmaEt runs the counter of an Ethiopian pharmacy: selling, receiving stock, counting,

>
> **Keeps selling through power and network cuts.** Every sale is saved on the phone first
> and uploads by itself when the connection returns. Days offline lose nothing.
>

> sold first, a warning before expired stock goes out, and counts that record who changed

> shortage is recorded against the shift, so the owner sees it.
>

> what their role allows. Each person signs in with their own PIN.
>
> **Private and secure.** Each pharmacy's records are isolated, all traffic is encrypted, and

> PharmaEt is for licensed pharmacies. Accounts are opened after we verify the pharmacy by

## 3. Google Play — Data safety form

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Does your app collect or share user data? | **Yes, collects. Does not share.** |
| Is all data encrypted in transit? | **Yes** |
| Can users request that data be deleted? | **Yes**: `https://<your-domain>/delete-account` |

Data types collected (none is shared, none is used for ads or tracking):

| Type | Collected | Required? | Purpose |
|---|---|---|---|
| Personal info → Name | Yes | Required | App functionality, Account management |
| Personal info → Phone number | Yes (owner, at account request) | Required | Account management |
| Personal info → User IDs | Yes (username) | Required | App functionality, Account management |
| Financial info → Purchase history | Yes (the pharmacy's sales) | Required | App functionality |
| Financial info → Other financial info | Yes (subscription payment screenshot) | Optional | Account management |
| Photos | Yes (only the screenshot the user picks) | Optional | Account management |
| App info and performance → Diagnostics | Yes (request logs, no content) | Required | App functionality |
| Device or other IDs | Yes (a random terminal id, not an advertising id) | Required | App functionality, Fraud prevention & security |

Not collected: location, contacts, messages, health info (no patient data is stored:
prescriptions are recorded by number, prescriber and date only), audio, calendar, web
browsing, advertising ID.

**Camera permission (added with FR-13).** The app asks for the camera when a user taps
"Scan a barcode". Frames are decoded on the device to read the barcode; no image or video is
stored or sent, so the camera adds **no** row to the table above. If the console asks why the
permission is requested: *"Scanning medicine barcodes at the pharmacy counter."* The camera
is declared not required, so phones without one can still install.

**Other Play Console declarations**
- *Ads:* No ads.
- *Target audience:* 18+ only; not designed for children.
- *Content rating (IARC):* Utility / business app. Answer **No** to violence, sexuality,
  language, controlled substances *(the app records dispensing for licensed pharmacies; it
  does not sell or promote drugs to users)*, gambling, user-generated content shared
  publicly. Expected rating: Everyone / 3+.
- *Health apps declaration:* "Medical/health business tools: pharmacy management", not a
  medical device, no diagnosis or treatment.
- *Government app:* No. *Financial features:* None (no payments processed in-app).
- *App access:* **Restricted.** Provide the review account from §5.

## 4. App Store — App Privacy ("nutrition label")

**Data used to track you:** None.
**Data linked to you** (all for *App Functionality*, some also *Account Management*):
Contact Info → Name, Phone Number · User Content → Photos (payment screenshot) · Identifiers
→ User ID · Purchases → Purchase History (the pharmacy's sales) · Diagnostics → Other
Diagnostic Data.
**Data not linked to you:** none beyond the above.

**Export compliance:** answered in the build. `ITSAppUsesNonExemptEncryption = NO` in
`Info.plist` (standard HTTPS and Keychain only).

**Sign in with Apple:** not required. Accounts are business accounts issued by PharmaEt
after verification, with no third-party or social login (guideline 4.8 exemption).

**Account deletion (5.1.1(v)):** More → Delete account (owners). Deletion is confirmed by a
call to the owner's registered number, which is permitted for regulated services and
explained on screen.

## 6. Owner checklist before the first submission

- [ ] The live environment from `../engineering/hosting.md`, ideally on your own domain
      (§11 there) before the store listing points its privacy URL at it.
- [ ] Build the store release against **that** URL: set the repository variable
      `MOBILE_API_BASE_URL=https://<your-domain>/api`. A release build refuses `http://`.
- [ ] Google Play: developer account ($25), create the app, turn on Play App Signing,
      upload the `.aab` from the CD artifact to *Internal testing* first.
- [ ] Apple: Developer Program ($99/year), the four secrets in `mobile-release.md` §3; the
      build then arrives in TestFlight by itself.
- [ ] Screenshots: at least 2 phone screenshots per store (Play: 16:9 or 9:16, 1080 px
      min; App Store: 6.7" 1290 × 2796 and 6.5" 1242 × 2688). Take them on the review tenant
      in both languages: sign-in, Sell, Stock, Cash-up, Reports, Help.
- [ ] Privacy policy and delete-account URLs open on your domain.
- [ ] Review the privacy policy wording, which is your legal commitment, and add a support
      email address if you want one listed.
