## 3. The five reasons owners buy it

Lead with whichever matches what they told you in §1.

### 1. "It never stops selling." *(power cuts, bad network)*
- Every sale is saved **on the phone first**. No internet? It keeps selling.
- When the network returns, everything uploads **by itself**. Nothing is lost, nothing is
  sent twice.
- *Show:* turn on airplane mode → make a sale → it works → turn data back on → the sync chip
  goes green.

### 2. "You will know if cash goes missing." *(trust in staff)*
- Each staff member signs in with **their own PIN**. Every sale, count and cash-up carries
  their name.
- **Open the till** with the morning cash; at the end, the cashier **counts the drawer**. The
  app shows *Balanced / Short / Over*, and the shortage is recorded against that person and
  that shift.
- Every **price change** records the old price, the new price and who changed it.
- *Show:* More → Cash up → enter a smaller amount → "Short", in red.

### 3. "No more expired medicine on the shelf." *(losses, inspections)*
- Every delivery is received with its **lot number and expiry date**.
- The app always sells the batch that **expires first** (FEFO).
- Home shows what **expires within 60 days**, so they can discount or return it in time.
- If the only stock left is expired, the app **warns the cashier**. Only the owner or a
  manager can authorise it, and that is recorded with their name.
- *Show:* Stock → Receive → add a box with an expiry date → Home → "Expiring within 60 days".

### 4. "See your pharmacy from anywhere." *(owners who are not always there)*
- **Reports** for today, the week or the month: sales by branch and by payment method,
  stock and expiry, and cash-up shortages by shift and by staff member.
- **Several branches** in one account. The owner sees all of them; a manager sees their own
  branch; a cashier sees what a cashier needs.
- *Show:* Reports → Today.

### 5. "It speaks Amharic, and it is ours." *(ease, fit)*
- The whole app in **Amharic or English**, switchable on the phone. Dates in the **Ethiopian
  calendar**.
- Built for Ethiopian pharmacies: birr, CBE and Telebirr, and EFDA-style records.
- A **step-by-step user guide** inside the app, in both languages, even before signing in.

## 6. Questions owners will ask — and honest answers

| **"What if the phone breaks or is stolen?"** | "Everything that synced is safe on our server. Sign in on a new phone and it comes back. Only sales made while offline and never synced stay on the old phone, so let it sync daily." |
| **"Can my staff cheat it?"** | "Every action is recorded with their name and cannot be quietly changed. Shortages show at cash-up, and price changes show who did them. You can remove a staff member's access in one tap." |

| **"iPhone?"** | "Android today. iPhone is coming." *(Do not promise a date.)* |
| **"Does it print receipts?"** | "Not yet; receipt printers are coming." *(Do not promise a date.)* |
| **"Can it record psychotropic / controlled medicines?"** | "The controlled-substance register is built and is being checked against EFDA's rules before we switch it on. Until then, keep your paper register for those." *(Never say it is approved.)* |
| **"Can customers pay by Telebirr?"** | "Yes, record it as a Telebirr/other payment. It is kept separate from the cash in the drawer, so the cash-up stays honest." |

## 7. Closing — sign them up on the spot (3 minutes)

1. **Ask:** "Shall we set up your pharmacy now? It takes five minutes."
   > «አሁን ፋርማሲዎን እናዘጋጅ? አምስት ደቂቃ ይወስዳል።»
2. **Send the APK** by WhatsApp/Telegram and install it on their phone
   (`INSTALL-ANDROID.md`: allow *Install unknown apps* → Install → *Install anyway*).
3. On their phone: **Request an account** → pharmacy name, owner name, phone, city,
   branches → Send.
4. On **your** phone, open the web console (https://pharmaet-2yw8.onrender.com) → **Sign-up
   requests** → **Approve**: choose their **pharmacy code** (e.g. `abebe-bole`), the owner's
   **username** and a **4–8 digit PIN**. Tell them the PIN **in person**.
5. They sign in → create their **first branch** → add their first products.
6. Show them **More → Subscription** with your CBE/Telebirr accounts. When they pay and
   upload the screenshot, approve it under **Payments** in the console (the screenshot is
   deleted after you decide; keep the box ticked).
7. Leave them with: "Call me on 0902432346 for anything. And the guide is under *More →
   Help*."
