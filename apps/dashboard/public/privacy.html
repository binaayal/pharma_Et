Privacy Policy

Effective 30 September 2026 · PharmaEt, Addis Ababa, Ethiopia

PharmaEt is software that pharmacies use to run their counter: sales, stock, cash-up and staff. This policy explains what we store, why, and what you can ask of us. We do not sell data, show advertising or track you across other apps or websites.

Who is responsible

PharmaEt, operated by Binyam Ayalneh Zerihun, Addis Ababa, Ethiopia. The pharmacy that uses PharmaEt is responsible for the business records it enters, and we process them on its behalf.

What we store, and why

InformationWhy
The pharmacy's name, city, number of branches, the owner's name and phone numberTo verify the pharmacy and open its account. We call this number to confirm who you are.
Staff names, usernames and rolesSo each person signs in and each sale, count and cash-up is recorded against who did it.
Products, prices, stock, lot numbers and expiry dates, sales, payments taken, cash-upsThis is the service: the pharmacy's own records.
For controlled medicines: prescription number, prescriber and prescription dateRequired to keep the controlled-substance register. We do not store patient names.
Payment screenshots you upload for your subscriptionSo a person can verify your payment. Stored encrypted and seen only by PharmaEt staff.
A random identifier for each phone, and technical logs (time, request, result) To keep sync reliable and the service secure. Logs contain no sales figures, prices or personal details.

We do not collect location, contacts, photos other than the screenshot you choose, or any advertising identifier.

How it is protected

All traffic is encrypted (HTTPS). PINs and passwords are stored only as secure hashes, never as themselves. Each pharmacy's records are isolated from every other pharmacy's in the database. On the phone, sign-in details are kept in the phone's secure storage (Android Keystore / iOS Keychain), and app data is excluded from backups.

Where it is stored

On our servers (Fly.io and Neon, in the European Union) and, for your own records, on your pharmacy's phones. We share data with no one except these hosting providers, who process it only to run the service, and authorities where Ethiopian law requires it.

How long we keep it

For as long as your pharmacy's account exists. Business and controlled-substance records may have to be kept for the period Ethiopian pharmacy regulations require, even after an account closes. Everything else is deleted when you ask us to (below).

Your choices

You can ask for a copy of your data, a correction, or deletion of your account. Owners can remove staff at any time from More → Branches & staff. To delete the pharmacy's account, see Delete your account.

Children

PharmaEt is a business tool for pharmacies and is not directed at children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes we will update the date above and tell account owners in the app. Questions: call or message PharmaEt support on 0902432346.
