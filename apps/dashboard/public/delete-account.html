Delete your account

How to delete a PharmaEt account, and what happens to the data.

A staff member's account

The pharmacy owner or manager removes it in the app: More → Branches & staff, open the person, tap Deactivate. They can no longer sign in. The sales they recorded stay in the pharmacy's records, because those records belong to the pharmacy.

The pharmacy's account

Only the owner can ask for this. In the app go to More → Delete account, or call PharmaEt support:

Call 0902432346

We confirm by calling the owner's registered phone number, so nobody else can close your pharmacy's account. Then, within 30 days:

  • Deleted: the owner's and staff members' names, usernames, phone numbers and sign-in details; payment screenshots; the pharmacy's product catalogue and prices.
  • Kept only where the law requires it: sales, stock and controlled-substance records that Ethiopian pharmacy regulations oblige the pharmacy to retain. These are kept for the legally required period, then deleted.

Uninstalling the app removes its data from that phone. Anything not yet synced is lost, so sync first if you want to keep it.

የሠራተኛ መለያ

የፋርማሲው መለያ

ይህን መጠየቅ የሚችለው ባለቤቱ ብቻ ነው። በመተግበሪያው ውስጥ ተጨማሪ → መለያ ሰርዝ ይሂዱ፣ ወይም የፋርማኢት ድጋፍን ይደውሉ፦
